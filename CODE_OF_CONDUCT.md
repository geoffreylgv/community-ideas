# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to provide a respectful, inclusive, and constructive environment for everyone participating in Community Ideas, regardless of background, experience level, language, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Welcome different perspectives and experience levels.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Keep discussions focused on the contribution, not on personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and do not publish confidential information.
- Communicate in English or French in a way that helps others participate.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Reporting concerns

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the repository maintainers privately through the contact method listed in the repository profile. Please include enough context for the maintainers to understand the concern.

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions that violate these guidelines and may restrict participation when necessary.
